START HERE highlight - how to post it

1. Post these as stories, in this order:
   1  story-1-start-here.mp4
   2  the showreel (10 - Showreel.mp4, in the main reels folder), as-is
   3  story-3-what-we-build.mp4
   4  story-4-how-it-works.mp4
   5  story-5-real-work.mp4
   6  story-6-your-move.mp4  -> add a Link sticker to realmsystems.net in the empty space
                               in the lower middle (it was left clear for it)

2. Tap "Highlight" on the first one, create a new highlight named: Start here
   and add the other five to it.

3. Cover: pick one when you create the highlight (Edit highlight > Edit cover):
   cover-start.png      orange R on ink (matches the reels)
   cover-start-alt.png  orange R on paper (matches the website)

Everything in these stories is true and from realmsystems.net. No prices anywhere.
Each story is 6 seconds with its own sound and starts on its own beat, so any one works alone.
