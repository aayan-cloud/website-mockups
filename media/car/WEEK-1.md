## 5. First replies (DMs)

The templates are in **DM REPLIES.txt** in the Drive folder "Realm Systems reels": what it is, the
price, pay on delivery, then "what's your business and city?". Send the matching one **first, with no
gate**, then let them talk. Copy it, and change the first line so it answers what they actually wrote.

- **BUILD:** they do not know what they need yet. Send the reply, then ask what eats their week and
  let them pick the service. No menu-pushing.
- **SITE:** quick and plain. If they already have a site, ask for the link and say one honest thing
  about it, never a teardown. Never promise rankings or more calls.
- **AGENT:** talk about their job, not the tech. Ask which task repeats every week, and say it the
  house way: it drafts, they approve, nothing goes to a customer without their tap.
- **CALLS:** calm, no scare numbers. Be exact that the text-back texts the caller back and does not
  answer with a voice; voice is the separate tier, built when someone signs.

**For all four:** reply the same day (check DMs at 5pm your time and before bed; their afternoon is
your night). If someone comments the keyword instead of DMing, reply "Sent you a message" and DM
them, because they acted first. Two follow-ups at most, a few days apart, then stop. Prices only ever
appear in these DMs. Instagram's saved replies can paste a template from a shortcut word if you want
to set that up yourself.
