Automate Upload

A command-line tool for putting my own finished videos into my own TikTok drafts.

What it does

I render short videos locally. This tool takes a finished file from my machine and sends it to my TikTok account as a draft, using TikTok's Content Posting API. I then open TikTok, look at the draft, and decide whether to publish it.

What it does not do

  • It does not post publicly on its own. Everything lands in drafts for a human to review.
  • It does not touch anyone else's account. One operator, one account.
  • It has no server, no sign-up and no users other than me.
  • It collects nothing. There is no analytics and nowhere for data to go.

How it handles credentials

The access and refresh tokens live in ~/.tiktok-cli.json on my own computer, readable only by my user account. They are sent to open.tiktokapis.com and nowhere else. Deleting that file removes them.

Permissions it asks for

  • user.info.basic — confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload — place a video in that account's drafts.
