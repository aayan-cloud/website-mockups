Privacy Policy

Automate Upload · last updated 20 September 2026

Automate Upload is a personal command-line tool. It uploads videos its own operator has made to that same operator's TikTok account. It is not a product, it has no other users, and it has no server.

What it collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no database and no backend to collect it with.

What it stores, and where

  • A TikTok access token and refresh token, written to ~/.tiktok-cli.json on the operator's own computer with owner-only file permissions. They never leave that machine except in requests to TikTok's own API.
  • Video files chosen by the operator, read from local disk and sent to TikTok. No copy is kept anywhere else.

What it sends, and to whom

Requests go to open.tiktokapis.com and nowhere else. No third party receives any data from this tool. Nothing is shared, sold, or used for advertising.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to place a video in that account's own drafts, where a human reviews and publishes it.

Retention and deletion

Deleting ~/.tiktok-cli.json removes every credential the tool holds. Access can also be revoked at any time from TikTok's own settings, under connected apps.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and has no public sign-up of any kind.
