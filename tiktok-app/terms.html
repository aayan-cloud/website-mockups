Automate Upload · last updated 20 September 2026

What this is

Automate Upload is a personal command-line tool that sends video files from its operator's computer to that operator's own TikTok account, as drafts. It is used by one person, offered to nobody, and sold to nobody.

Acceptable use

  • Only content the operator made or has the rights to publish.
  • Only the operator's own TikTok account, authorised through TikTok's own OAuth screen.
  • Uploads go to drafts. A human reviews each one before it is published.
  • TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service apply to everything posted.

Rate limits

The tool respects TikTok's published posting limits and does not attempt to exceed them or to work around any restriction TikTok applies to an account.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, with no warranty. TikTok's API can change or become unavailable at any time, and the operator accepts that risk.

Termination

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's connected-apps settings, or by deleting the local credential file. No account exists here to close.
